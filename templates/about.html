About TravelGenius AI

An enterprise-grade, modern generative AI web application engineered to democratize personalized holiday planning.

Our Engineering Vision

Traditional travel planning is fragmented and overwhelming. Travelers spend an average of 10 to 20 hours navigating disparate travel forums, blog posts, booking portals, and transit timetables.

TravelGenius AI solves this by pairing Google’s latest Gemini large language models with structured data pipelines. Users provide their origin, destination, budget in INR, dates, travel style, and dietary requirements. The AI synthesizes a comprehensive, time-blocked holiday schedule with morning, afternoon, and evening activities, gastronomy highlights, neighborhood suggestions, transit tips, and weather backups.

Technology Stack

Backend Core

Python 3.14 & Flask microframework. RESTful architecture with JSON validation and modular services.

Generative AI Engine

Google Gemini API via official google-genai SDK and REST fallback with strict JSON schemas.

Local Persistence

SQLite 3 engine with zero-dependency lightweight relational storage for saving and managing trips.

Frontend & UI/UX

HTML5, CSS3, Bootstrap 5.3, Font Awesome 6.5, Vanilla JS, and responsive glassmorphism design tokens.

Responsible AI & Transparency

  • No Fake Live Inventories: We do not fabricate live flight tickets, room availability, or real-time booking statuses. All costs are framed as estimates in INR.
  • Explicit Demo Mode Labeling: If a Gemini API key is missing or unreachable, the system transparently labels generated itineraries as high-fidelity samples so users are never misled.
  • Privacy-First Architecture: Trip details are stored locally on your machine/server in SQLite. No personal travel itineraries are sold or tracked.
